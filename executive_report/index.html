What to investigate first

Route 58 and repeat crash locations

Screen the corridor for repeat crashes. Match points to the road network and collect turning volumes before selecting junctions for intervention.

Nighttime safety

Review full-night fatal crashes for recurring locations. Check site lighting, speeds and nighttime traffic volumes.

Pedestrian crossings

Review fatal pedestrian crash sites and nearby crossings. Record pedestrian volumes, crossing layouts and visibility.

Data scope

Japan’s National Police Agency open data covers police-reported crashes involving injury or death. The Okinawa subset covers one year and has no entry for June 13. Comparing risk across places or times requires traffic or pedestrian volumes. The fatal-crash share describes crashes that have already occurred.
